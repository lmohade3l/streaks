Habit PWA · v1

Streaks

Today's habits, one-tap completion, and a create-habit sheet. It's live — tap the rings, add a habit, watch the counts move.

Assumptions

Single user, offline-first, installed to the home screen. One screen carries the daily job; history and settings sit a level deeper. Calm neutral canvas so a single green is the only thing that says “done”.

Decisions

Streaks are quiet numerals with a seven-day dot trail — no fire, no confetti. Count habits (water, glasses) tap up one at a time and complete themselves at target. The form is a bottom sheet: thumb-reachable, and the list stays visible behind it.

Open questions

Does a missed day break the streak at midnight, or is there a grace day? Any notion of rest days for weekday-only habits? Want a week/month history view next?
